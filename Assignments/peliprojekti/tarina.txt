In the year 2083, Finland has banned the usage of salmiakki, because it's too reminiscent of intoxicating substances.
Now the only way to get salmiakki is from the Haaparanta candy store, but the prices have skyrocketed since the ban.
One single piece of salmiakki now costs 100 €

Your mission is to travel through Finland and reach the Haaparanta candy store collecting enough money on the way. 

The only option for travel is walking.
Motorized vehicles can not be used, because global warming has reached a critical point
at which any greenhouse gasses will cause global destruction.
