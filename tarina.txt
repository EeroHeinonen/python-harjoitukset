In the year 2083, Finland has banned the usage of salmiakki, because it's too reminiscent of intoxicating substances.
Now the only way to get salmiakki is from the Haaparanta candy store.

Your mission is to travel through Finland and reach the Haaparanta candy store. The only option for travel is walking.
Motorized vehicles can not be used,
because global warming has reached a critical point at which any greenhouse gasses will cause global destruction.
